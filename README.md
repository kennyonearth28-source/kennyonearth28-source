### 🧭 About me

- 🛠️ I'm building **SOL Desk**, a desk for SOL perpetuals staffed by AI agents: research, a confluence gate, a paper trader, and a reviewer.
- 🤖 The agents read the chart, score the setup, paper it, and review it. They never sign a transaction.
- 🖱️ Every live trade is a human click from a connected wallet.
- 📓 Paper goes first. The strategy has to prove itself on the paper book before it earns real size.
- 🌐 Live at **[soldesk.fun](https://soldesk.fun)**

### 🔨 What I'm building

**SOL Desk** trades one playbook, the **TJR / ICT 3/3 confirm**. Bias first, then all three confirms:

| Step | What has to be true |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HTF bias** | Higher-timeframe direction is set first. No bias, no ticket. |
| **1 · Liquidity sweep** | Price runs a prior high or low and takes the liquidity. |
| **2 · Break of structure** | Structure breaks in the bias direction after the sweep. |
| **3 · POI** | Entry comes from a fair value gap (FVG) or order block (OB). |

How the desk runs it:

- 🔎 **Research agents**: Rowe runs the TJR scan and writes the bias sheet, Voss watches the tape and the red-folder calendar, Jules marks levels.
- 🧠 **Boss, the confluence gate**: a ticket only moves when the HTF bias and all 3 confirms line up.
- 📒 **Atlas, the paper perps book**: takes gated setups on paper with a real stop, so every idea has a record.
- 🧾 **Quill, the reviewer**: grades the paper book for process, not P&L.
- 🔔 **Alerts**: a phone ping when a gated SOL perp ticket is ready.
- ✋ **Human-only execution**: no agent places a live order. I decide, and I click.
